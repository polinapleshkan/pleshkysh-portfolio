Questions

Before you ask.

Can foreigners buy property in Dubai?

Yes. In designated freehold areas, which cover Dubai Marina, Downtown, Business Bay, and most of the neighborhoods we list, foreign nationals can own property outright, with full title registered at the Dubai Land Department.

How does buying off-plan actually work?

You reserve a unit with a booking deposit, then pay the rest in installments tied to construction milestones set by the developer. Most launches ask for 10-20% upfront, with the balance paid over the build period and the final payment due on handover.

Do you charge a commission?

For resale purchases, the standard agency fee in Dubai is 2% of the sale price, paid by the buyer on completion. Off-plan reservations are commission-free for you, since the developer pays us directly.

Is there tax on the property or the rental income?

No income tax on rent and no capital gains tax on resale. It's one of the reasons Dubai keeps attracting overseas buyers.

Do you help with financing?

We're not a bank, but we can put you in front of mortgage brokers we trust and tell you honestly whether a project is likely to get financed before you commit a deposit.

What if I can't view in person?

We do live video walkthroughs via WhatsApp or a phone call, and can send extra photos or floor plans of anything listed on our website. Several of our clients bought without setting foot in Dubai before handover.

How do I book a viewing?

Pick a day and a time in the booking widget above, or message us directly on WhatsApp. An agent will call you to confirm.

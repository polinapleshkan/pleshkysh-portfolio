The studio

Two founders, one senior designer, no account managers.

Isla and Tom founded the studio in 2013 after years of work going out with other people's names on the door. Greta joined in 2019 and now runs a third of our projects day to day, with a director still on every one.

Isla Fenn

Founder & Creative Director

Trained at Central Saint Martins, ran the interiors desk at a Mayfair practice for six years before starting the studio.

Tom Wren

Founder & Architectural Director

RIBA-chartered, previously led planning submissions at a Clerkenwell architecture practice for five years.

Greta Lindqvist

Senior Interior Designer

Came from a hospitality studio in 2019 and brought the members' club work with her. Runs delivery on the projects she isn't designing herself.

What we do

Four ways to start.

01

Interior Design

Full furnishing schemes for homes, from spatial planning to final styling. We work with your architect, or bring our own.

02

Architecture

Extensions, loft conversions and full renovations, with planning and building-regulations drawings handled in-house.

03

Turnkey & Project Management

We hire the trades, track the budget and stay on site so you don't have to. You call us, not five different trades.

04

Styling & Sourcing

Furniture, art and finishing touches sourced from the workshops and auction houses we've used for over a decade.

How it works

Four stages, one point of contact.

1

Consultation

We visit the space, talk through how you actually live and give you an honest read on budget and timeline.

2

Concept

Mood boards, spatial layouts and a materials palette, presented in person, not emailed as a PDF.

3

Design Development

Detailed drawings, specifications and supplier quotes, refined until every line is signed off.

4

Delivery

Site visits, snagging and styling on installation day, so it looks exactly like the drawing.

Colour we keep returning to

Six pairs we actually specify.

Six pairings we come back to. Every one of them has been on a wall, a sofa or a run of joinery in the last two years.

Oxblood & Olive

#5F1F2C / #8A8E52

Deepest red in the range. Best on joinery where the light is poor and the room can carry it.

Raspberry & Sky

#A82F47 / #B7CDDB

The blue keeps the red from turning sentimental. We use it in small rooms that need waking up.

Cocoa & Blush

#4B342A / #E0A9A0

Brown does the structural work here; pink only trims it. Reads warmer under lamplight than daylight.

Olive & Walnut

#8A8E52 / #43301F

Sits quietly next to real timber, which is the point. Specified where the wood is already in the room.

Cream & Lilac

#EFE6D2 / #8E7FA3

The lightest pair we hold. Lilac stays chalky, so keep it off a gloss finish.

Damson & Mustard

#3E2233 / #C08A2E

Two colours that argue slightly. Worth it in a dining room, or on a staircase people pass through rather than sit in.
